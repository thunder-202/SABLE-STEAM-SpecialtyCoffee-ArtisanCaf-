Coffee Philosophy · Small-Lot Roasting

Every Cup Has a Story.

Great coffee begins long before steam rises from the cup. At SABLE & STEAM, we partner with seasonal micro-lot growers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Papua New Guinea, roasting lightly in small batches in Sydney to honour the distinct terroir of every harvest.

Whether pulling a syrupy morning ristretto or hand-pouring a floral washed filter, our baristas weigh, time, and taste throughout service—treating daily coffee not as a hurried transaction, but as a quiet craft.

01. Seasonal Direct-Trade Lots

Single-estate harvests selected at peak sweetness and rotated with the Australian seasons.

02. Tailored Water & Roast Profiles

Custom remineralised Sydney water paired with gentle roast curves that preserve origin clarity.

03. Unhurried Bar Service

Served in hand-thrown New South Wales stoneware warmed specifically for milk or black coffee.

Our Story · Established in Surry Hills

Good Coffee. Thoughtful Moments.

Shaped by Sydney’s sunlit café culture and a quiet appreciation for tactile architecture, SABLE & STEAM was imagined as a sanctuary between home and work.

Craft & Provenance

Built around patience, transparency, and warm hospitality.

SABLE & STEAM began with a simple observation: Sydney produces some of the finest espresso in the world, yet the spaces where we drink it have grown increasingly hurried. We set out to restore a sense of calm ritual to the morning cup—pairing precision extraction with natural materials that age gracefully.
From hand-trowelled limewash plaster and muted olive banquettes to ceramics glazed by independent makers in the Southern Highlands, every detail is chosen for warmth rather than spectacle. Whether you stay for five quiet minutes at the espresso bar or an hour at the oak communal table, you are warmly welcome.
